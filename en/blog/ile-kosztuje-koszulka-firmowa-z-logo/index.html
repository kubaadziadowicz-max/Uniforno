How much does a company t-shirt with a logo cost?

Price list and the factors that affect price (2026)

It’s one of the first questions companies looking for logo clothing ask. The answer “it depends” can be frustrating — so we give concrete ranges and explain what really drives the price. With us, every quote is individual, but the figures below give a realistic reference point.

Quick answer

A company t-shirt with a logo is roughly from 85 PLN (approx. €20) net per piece for an order of 30, and from 70 PLN (approx. €16) for 100. The price depends on the material, cut, branding method and order size.

Why it’s worth paying for quality

The prices above are for premium clothing — thick, certified knit fabrics from Polish knitting mills, sewn in our workshop and branded with a method that withstands hundreds of washes. These aren’t thin t-shirts with a print that cracks after a few washes. You pay for clothing the team enjoys wearing for years — not a cost that comes back a few months later as a replacement.

What determines the final price?

Material type and weight

a thicker, better knit fabric costs more but lasts longer.

Cut

a fitted model, a half-zip or a classic t-shirt involve different levels of work.

Branding method

printing is included. Embroidery may carry a surcharge, depending on the complexity of the graphic (number of colours, size, logo detail).

Order size

the larger the run, the lower the unit price.

Embroidery or printing — which to choose?

Both methods have their uses. Printing works well for colourful, complex graphics; embroidery gives a more prestigious, durable result on polos and sweatshirts. We cover the differences in a separate article — embroidery vs printing — which to choose.

What’s the minimum quantity?

What is the minimum order quantity? We take orders from 30 pieces. This run size lets us keep full control over the quality of the cut and the branding.
