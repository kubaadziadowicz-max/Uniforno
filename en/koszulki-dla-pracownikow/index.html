Employer branding starts with the details

Companies invest tens of thousands in recruitment, employer branding and organisational culture. Meanwhile the company t-shirt — one of the simplest, most visible identity elements — is often ordered on a cheapest-possible basis.

A cheap t-shirt sends employees a signal: your comfort doesn’t matter to us. A premium t-shirt says something entirely different — we care about the details, even the everyday ones. It’s a non-verbal message that builds organisational culture.

Uniforno designs employee t-shirts for companies across a range of industries: technology, services, manufacturing and retail. The common denominator: every t-shirt is made from premium Polish knit fabrics and has a cut that looks good.

What sets our employee t-shirts apart

Premium knit fabrics: cotton at 180–240 g/m², soft, durable, holding its shape after hundreds of washes. There’s no room here for pilling, deformation after the first wash or ink that fades within a month.

A cut made to the client: you don’t pick from a catalogue of S–XXL sizes and one model. We talk about how your company’s t-shirt should look — relaxed or fitted, with a collar or without, what length — and sew to those guidelines.

Premium branding: computerised embroidery is our speciality for employee clothing. Durable, elegant, it doesn’t crumble in the wash. Ideal for logos with fine lines and detailed graphics.

What working with a company looks like

We work with HR departments, office managers and marketing teams. We understand that ordering employee t-shirts is a project with many variables: different sizes, onboarding-related deadlines and the need to top up stock.

We offer a B2B model with a dedicated account manager who keeps to deadlines and is available throughout the whole process. For companies with regular orders we prepare individual terms.

We take orders from 30 pieces. Standard lead time: 10–14 business days. We deliver across Poland.
