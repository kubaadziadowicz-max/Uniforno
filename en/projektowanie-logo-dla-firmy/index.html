Have a logo but in the wrong format? We’ll vectorise it

A common scenario in the B2B sector is having a logo in an unsuitable, amateur format. JPG files, screenshots, tiny PNGs with a background or old scans make professional fabric branding completely impossible. Both precise computerised embroidery and advanced digital printing require clean lines and curves.

For a sewing or printing machine to apply the design onto the knit fabric correctly and sharply, the logo must be professionally prepared for printing. This is where logo vectorisation comes in. It’s a technical process of completely redrawing your pixel file into a scalable vector format. A vector built this way can be enlarged freely without the slightest loss of quality.

Just as with creating a mark from scratch, logo vectorisation is a service you can commission from us entirely on its own. We’ll effectively fix your file and hand you a fully-fledged vector.

If you choose to produce clothing at the same time, keeping to our minimum of 30 pieces, the technical preparation of the logo for print or embroidery is included in the whole order. Your company’s image flaws are permanently fixed with no extra charge for graphic-studio work.

What does the process look like?

We respect business owners’ time. We’ve reduced our process to reliable, logistical essentials. Whether you choose logo design alone or combine it with production of our own clothing, we start with a quick exchange of information.

You describe your main line of business and any visual preferences. If you have a poor-quality file — you simply send it to us. The quote for your inquiry is entirely free. You’ll receive a clear offer in your inbox in exactly 24 hours.

Once the terms are approved, we get to work on the graphics right away. We deliver a technically correct company logo design. If the service is linked to production, we start the workshop after the graphic is approved. We select the right weight of Polish knit fabrics, set the cut of the garments and apply the new mark to the fabric.

The entire production process takes place in Poland. This gives us rigorous control over the final quality of the product. We pack the finished garments and send them by courier straight to your office, while also handing over the final vector files.
