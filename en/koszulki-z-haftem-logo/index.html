Why companies choose embroidery over printing

The difference between classic printing and thread is enormous. Printing is mass production. Over time it cracks, fades and crumbles under high temperatures. Embroidered company t-shirts are an unambiguous premium signal. It’s a branding technique reserved for brands that insist on the highest quality of detail.

Embroidery survives everything. It endures hundreds of wash cycles in demanding conditions, keeping its original colour and dense structure. The thread doesn’t degrade during ironing. It’s absolutely the most damage-resistant method of branding fabric.

When an employee does physical work, clothing wears out fast. Poor-quality branding damages your image far faster than having no logo at all. A ruined logo suggests carelessness to clients. T-shirts with a stitched logo, by contrast, look impeccable throughout their multi-year life.

It’s a cool business calculation. Instead of replacing faded t-shirts every quarter, you invest capital once in solid clothing. That’s exactly why managers who expect a long-term return on their image investment choose it.

Above all, you gain full independence from off-the-shelf semi-finished goods. Most advertising agencies buy thin Chinese catalogue t-shirts and simply apply the client’s graphic to them. Uniforno has a different business model. We make your clothing.
