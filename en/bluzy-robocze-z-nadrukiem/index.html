Why a cheap workwear sweatshirt is an expensive mistake

Buying workwear based solely on the lowest unit price is a classic financial trap. Clothing like this wears out instantly in an operational environment. Thin material quickly frays, loses its original structure after just a few washes, and poor-quality ink cracks and flakes unattractively in the cold.

As a result, cheap clothing lasts barely a single season. This forces the company to reorder constantly every few months, generating hidden administrative and logistical costs.

When you calculate total cost of ownership (TCO), the picture reverses dramatically. A professional printed workwear sweatshirt made by Uniforno easily withstands years of heavy use and hundreds of demanding industrial wash cycles. Instead of buying three low-quality garments over three years, you invest capital once in a very solid product. In the long term, this solution is far more cost-effective for the company budget.

A ruined, crumbling logo on a fitter’s back subconsciously suggests to the client that the services themselves are done carelessly. Durable applications on our knit fabrics prevent image losses and guarantee full visual consistency across the whole team throughout years of use.
