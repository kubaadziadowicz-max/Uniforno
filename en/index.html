Our philosophy

Most of the market is a quick print on thin, off-the-shelf t-shirts. We create premium clothing from scratch. We make pieces that survive years of heavy use and that people wear by choice — long after the workday or the event is over.

Premium quality

Clothing people want to wear

Our clothing doesn’t end up at the bottom of the wardrobe after the first wash. We sew from high-weight Polish knit fabrics, caring for every seam and finish. These are garments employees put on by choice — after hours too, long after the workday or event is over. Premium quality you can feel from the first touch.

Designed for you

A cut designed for you

We create a unique cut from scratch, or propose proven solutions — you decide how much freedom to leave us. We match the fit, weight and finish precisely to your company’s character. Oversize, slim or classic cut, collar or none, length and details — we settle everything together and produce it in a Polish workshop with full control over every stage.

Durable embroidery and print

Computerised embroidery and modern printing that survive hundreds of washes without cracking or fading. We match the branding technique to the specifics of your logo and material — so your mark looks flawless throughout the life of the garment.
