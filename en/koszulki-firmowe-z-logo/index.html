Made-to-order t-shirts: printing or embroidery

We apply the logo with two techniques. Printing (screen printing or DTG) works well for colourful graphics and larger runs — it gives vivid colours at a sensible unit cost. Embroidery is a more prestigious, durable form of branding, ideal for polos and heavier models. We match the method to your design and volume. (Want embroidery only? See embroidered logo t-shirts.)

Why made-to-order printed t-shirts, not wholesale blanks

Off-the-shelf wholesale t-shirts are usually made of lower-quality fabric that loses all its appeal after a few washes — it fades, loses shape and pills. We select proven, premium Polish knit fabrics for the intended use and watch over sewing quality and branding durability, so the t-shirt still looks good after dozens of washes. It’s a difference you can see and feel — especially over time.

What working together looks like

Step 01

Brief & conversation

You fill in the form or call us. We talk about your needs, quantity, timeline and what your company clothing should express.

Step 02

Design & visualisation

We prepare the cut and branding design. You receive a visualisation for approval — at no cost at this stage.

Step 03

Visualisation for approval

Before production starts, the visualisation lets you confirm the placement of the logo and prints, the colour choices and the whole composition. You approve or request changes, and we apply them until the final version — once you approve, the garments go to print.

Step 04

Production & delivery

Once approved, production begins. Turnaround is 10–21 business days. We deliver to your chosen address — and, if needed, we can ship to multiple addresses.
