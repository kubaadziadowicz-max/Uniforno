Why a polo, not an ordinary t-shirt?

The polo is the absolute golden mean in a company dress code. It’s the ideal compromise between an ordinary t-shirt and a formal shirt. It looks highly professional while completely removing the uncomfortable need to wear a tie. It’s clothing that effectively builds authority without creating artificial distance between an employee and a client.

This wardrobe item performs superbly in absolutely any conditions. The polo passes the tough test of work in a modern office. It’s perfect for fieldwork and for direct, everyday contact with clients. It’s also a reliable choice for industry events and trade fairs.

Company polos are an undeniable classic of corporate clothing. Their timeless character is a huge business advantage. This cut simply never goes out of fashion. It secures the company’s image for years, removing the need to constantly redesign the staff’s clothing.

A firm, precisely sewn collar and the characteristic button placket instantly raise the status of the outfit. An employee equipped with a high-class polo immediately commands more respect from business partners.

Embroidery or print on a polo shirt?

The right branding method determines how the brand is ultimately perceived. Piqué knit has a distinctive, raised mesh structure. This material is a natural environment for classic computerised embroidery. A polo’s collar automatically draws the other person’s eye to the chest and perfectly showcases an embroidered logo.

Thick, dense thread on a piqué weave looks far more prestigious than branding on an ordinary, smooth t-shirt. Embroidery guarantees the highest durability available. An embroidered design withstands hundreds of industrial wash cycles. It doesn’t crack in the cold and doesn’t deform under the heat of an iron.

Printed company polos, on the other hand, we recommend for specific, advanced projects. It’s the optimal choice for complex graphics with smooth tonal transitions. If your brand book requires a particular gradient to be reproduced, we use reliable digital techniques. A durable print keeps full flexibility and strong colour saturation.

Whichever technological route you choose, we deliver flawlessly made logo polos. We advise the best solution immediately after analysing the vector file. Durable company logo polos for employees have to withstand many months of use in varied conditions, and we allow no mistakes here.
